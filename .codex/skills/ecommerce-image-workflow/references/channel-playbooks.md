# Channel Playbooks

Use this reference when a user names a marketplace, ad channel, social surface, or batch export requirement.

## Marketplace Listings

Prioritize factual clarity and product fidelity.

- Use clean white, light gray, or simple contextual backgrounds depending on platform allowance.
- Keep the first image focused on the sellable product and included quantity.
- Limit image text unless the channel allows it.
- Avoid badges, claims, guarantees, or comparison language that the user has not approved.
- Create secondary images for features, dimensions, included items, lifestyle use, and details.

## Paid Social

Prioritize thumb-stopping clarity while preserving product accuracy.

- Build variants around one changing variable: audience, hook, setting, season, background color, or offer.
- Reserve safe space for short approved copy.
- Use lifestyle context when it helps the buyer recognize the use case.
- Keep product large enough to recognize on mobile.
- Avoid overloading the frame with many benefits.

## Short-Video Thumbnails

Prioritize instant recognition and high contrast.

- Use one large product subject or face/product pairing when allowed.
- Keep copy to a few approved words.
- Use strong foreground/background separation.
- Avoid small UI-like details that disappear at feed size.

## Storefront And Landing Pages

Prioritize brand consistency and visual system reuse.

- Define a repeatable background, lighting, and crop system.
- Use image families: hero, feature bands, comparison modules, testimonials, and FAQ visuals.
- Keep product details inspectable in the first viewport.
- Export both desktop and mobile crops when layout will change.

## Batch Naming

Use a naming pattern that keeps variants reviewable:

```text
<sku>_<channel>_<shot-type>_<angle-or-hook>_<locale>_<ratio>_v<number>.<ext>
```

Example:

```text
sku123_meta_feature_cooling-en-us_4x5_v03.png
```
